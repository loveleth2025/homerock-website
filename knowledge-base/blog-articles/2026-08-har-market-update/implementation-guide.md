HOW TO USE THESE 4 BLOG ARTICLES (Sellers, Buyers, Investors, Realtors)

OPTION 1: SEGMENT-SPECIFIC BLOGS (Recommended)
- Publish 4 separate blog posts on your website
- URL structure: 
  • nataliepilkinton.com/blog/sell-home-houston-balanced-market
  • nataliepilkinton.com/blog/buy-home-houston-2026-advantage
  • nataliepilkinton.com/blog/real-estate-investment-houston-2026
  • nataliepilkinton.com/blog/realtor-guide-balanced-market-strategy

- Post each on different weeks (spread out over 1 month)
- Each drives traffic to segment-specific landing page (Seller CTA, Buyer CTA, Investor CTA)

OPTION 2: INTEGRATED MEGA-POST
- Combine all 4 into one 12,000+ word "Ultimate Houston Market Guide 2026"
- Use header navigation (jump links) to navigate between segments
- Better SEO (more content, more keywords)
- Lower bounce rate (readers stay on page)

- URL: nataliepilkinton.com/blog/houston-real-estate-market-2026-complete-guide

OPTION 3: SEGMENT + MEGA (Best)
- Publish 4 segment posts individually (each optimized for segment keywords)
- Publish 1 mega-post that links to all 4
- Cross-link between posts (internal links boost SEO)
- Drive traffic to segments from mega-post

SEO OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST

**ON-PAGE SEO:**
☐ Title tag (60 chars max): Primary keyword + brand
  Example: "Sell Your Houston Home Fast 2026 | Natalie Pilkinton Real Estate"

☐ Meta description (160 chars): HAR data mention + benefit + CTA hint
  Example: "HAR June 2026 data shows balanced market. Learn how to price right, sell faster in Spring, Woodlands, Katy. Free seller strategy guide inside."

☐ H1 tag: Matches title (or very similar)
  Example: "Sell Your Houston Home Fast in 2026: Balanced Market Strategy for Greater Houston, Spring, The Woodlands, and Katy"

☐ H2s: Subheadings with keywords naturally incorporated
  Example: "HAR June 2026 Data Tells Us About the Houston Market"

☐ Keyword density: Primary keyword appears 3-5 times (naturally)
  "sell Houston, Houston real estate, selling homes in Houston"

☐ LSI keywords (related keywords): Naturally throughout
  "real estate market Houston, HAR data, home prices Houston, Spring homes, Woodlands homes, Katy homes, Sugar Land homes"

☐ Image ALT text: Describe image + keyword (not keyword stuffing)
  Example: "HAR June 2026 chart showing balanced market inventory in Houston"

☐ Internal links: Link to other blog posts, landing pages
  Example: Link to buyer landing page from sellers post ("Buyers have advantage" → buyer CTA)

☐ External links: Link to HAR.com, authority sources
  Example: "According to HAR.com's June 2026 Housing Market Update..."

☐ Call-to-action: Button/link in conclusion + newsletter form
  Example: "[Schedule Free Home Valuation]" for sellers

**TECHNICAL SEO:**
☐ URL structure: Keywords in URL
  ✓ nataliepilkinton.com/blog/sell-home-houston-balanced-market
  ✗ nataliepilkinton.com/blog/article-123

☐ Page speed: <3 second load time (images compressed, minimal scripts)

☐ Mobile-friendly: Responsive design, text readable on mobile

☐ SSL certificate: HTTPS (not HTTP)

☐ Structured data: Use schema.org markup (especially for business, address, review if applicable)
