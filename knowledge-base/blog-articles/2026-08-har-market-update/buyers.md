SECTION 2: The Buyer Advantage in Balanced Markets

[H2]

What does "balanced market advantage" actually MEAN for your offer, negotiation, and deal?

**ADVANTAGE #1: You Can Negotiate Inspections**
In a seller's market (2022-2023), buyers waived home inspections to win bidding wars. Crazy. Now?
- Standard inspection: 10 days (you pay ~$400-500)
- You KEEP the inspection contingency (if inspector finds $15K in repairs, you can renegotiate or walk)
- Sellers EXPECT this now. They don't see inspections as threats; they expect them.

Your money: Protected. Peace of mind: Secured.

**ADVANTAGE #2: Appraisal Contingency Is Normal**
If the home appraises at $380K but you agreed to pay $395K, you're covered.
- You get renegotiation room (don't pay $15K above appraisal without cause)
- Seller can't force you to make up the difference
- In a balanced market, sellers accept this. In a hot market, they didn't.

Your equity: Safer.

**ADVANTAGE #3: Closing Cost Negotiation Is Open**
Sellers expect to negotiate closing costs (2-3% of purchase price = $7-12K on $350K home).
- Ask for seller credits toward closing costs
- Sellers will often pay 1-2% to close the deal
- In hot markets, sellers wouldn't budge. Now they do.

Your cash at closing: Preserved.

**ADVANTAGE #4: Time to Find The Right Home**
You're not competing for the 1-2 homes on the market. There are 20-30 in your price range. You can:
- Search carefully without fear of missing out
- Schedule inspections without rushing
- Get pre-approval without feeling pressured
- Ask detailed questions at showings

Your decision-making: Thoughtful, not panicked.

**ADVANTAGE #5: Rates Are Lower Than Last Year**
A buyer who waited from June 2025 to June 2026 gets:
- 6.44% rate (instead of 6.82%)
- $60-80/month savings
- $7,200/year kept vs. 2025
- On $500K loan over 30 years: ~$32,000 total savings

Waiting paid off. And rates are stabilizing (not swinging wildly), so you're not missing the last rate drop.

Your cost of money: Minimized.

□ If appraisal < offer price, you can renegotiate
□ Timeline: 10-14 days typically
□ Cost: Included in lender fees (usually)

**STEP 9: FINAL WALKTHROUGH (Day before closing)**
□ Verify agreed-upon repairs were done
□ Check home is in expected condition
□ Confirm appliances/fixtures are included
□ Timeline: 1-2 hours
□ Cost: FREE

**STEP 10: CLOSE (Day 45-60)**
□ Sign final paperwork
□ Get keys
□ You own the home
□ Timeline: 1-2 hours at closing table
□ Cost: Closing costs (2-5% of purchase price, negotiated with seller or financed)

TOTAL TIMELINE: 45-60 days from offer to keys in hand.

SECTION 7: Timing - When to Buy in 2026

[H2]

**BEST TIME:** July-September (Summer end)
- Inventory still high
- Less competition from other buyers (back-to-school busy)
- End-of-summer movers motivated
- School year just started (some buyers ruled out)
- YOUR ADVANTAGE: More homes available, fewer offers/home

**SECOND BEST:** January-February
- Motivated movers (New Year goals, job transfers)
- Inventory lower but demand much lower
- Less competition but fewer homes

**AVOID:** March-April (Spring surge)
- Inventory UP but buyer competition UP more
- Spring movers, renovators, investors all bidding
- More offers/home, prices higher

**ALSO GOOD:** October-November
- Inventory still available
- Holiday season = fewer buyers (their loss)
- Fewer showings, less bidding pressure

Conclusion: BUY NOW (July-September). Don't wait for spring 2027. Balanced markets don't turn into better buyer markets; they turn into hot markets.
